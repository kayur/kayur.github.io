I work at Meta Super Intelligence Labs across design, engineering, and product — finding ways to make AI more useful and easier to use. I have been asking the same question for twenty years: why is building software with machine learning still so hard?

Background
Meta
Joined Meta Super Intelligence Labs after the acquisition of Malleable. Work across design, engineering, and product on next-generation AI experiences. Built a prototyping team and explored multiplayer and contextual AI.
Malleable
Co-founded Malleable to explore how AI could make groups of people more effective. Built frontier prototypes in deep research, UI generation, and multiplayer AI collaboration. Acquired by Meta before launch.
Apple
Worked with the design studio to understand how machine learning shapes user experience. Created ML-based products, presented at WWDC 2019, and released the Human Interface Guidelines for Machine Learning.
Google
Founded and led the Colaboratory team at Google Research. Taught some of the first large-scale ML and data science courses at Google and at Columbia University.
Univ. of Washington
Studied how machine learning engineers actually work, then built tools to make their jobs easier. Wrote a thesis whose findings are still relevant a decade later.
Stanford
ML-focused masters. Happened to be in the room when Stanford decided to build a car for the DARPA Grand Challenge — wrote the first Stanford paper on self-driving cars. Also studied physical skill learning in VR.
Carnegie Mellon
Computer science, machine learning, and design. Worked on speech systems on wearable computers with head-mounted displays. Set the groundwork for everything since.
